### INC-20 — Ministry of Education

- **Label:** CONFIRMED HISTORICAL INCIDENT
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Incident date:** 2024-04-04
- **Publication date:** 2024-04-04
- **System concerned:** Website
- **What was reported:** The Ministry's official website was accessed without authorization and a message was posted on it.
- **Confirmed by an official body:** The Ministry acknowledged the incident and apologized to users (6 April 2024). Sri Lanka CERT's senior information security engineer, as reported by Daily Mirror on 10 April 2024, said CERT's initial investigation indicated a local individual was responsible and that a report would go to the Minister. The same article reports that CERT had advised the Ministry to carry out a vulnerability assessment annually and that this had not been done, and that CERT recommended launching a new website instead of reinstating the old one.
- **Reported allegations and unconfirmed details:** The article also says necessary security patches and updates had not been applied, without attributing the statement to a named source. The posted message claimed to come from a student and asserted security flaws; the author's identity was not established. A Ministry official told Ceylon Today that no Ministry information was compromised. Sri Lanka CERT's own statement or report was not found.
- **Official investigation announced:** A statement reported on 6 April 2024 said Sri Lanka CERT and the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission were leading a probe. On 10 April 2024 CERT was reported as saying its findings would go to the Minister and then to the CID.
- **Investigation outcome:** Unknown
- **Remediation confirmed:** A Ministry official said the site was restored on 5 April 2024. On 10 April 2024 the site was reported as due to return with basic features. Whether a new website was later launched, as CERT recommended, was not found.
- **Source type:** News report of official statement
- **Sources:** [source](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/SLCERT-engineers-reports-to-be-sent-to-Education-Minister-today/108-280550), [supporting source](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Probe-underway-into-breach-of-Education-Ministry-website/108-280302), [further source 1](https://ceylontoday.lk/2024/04/06/education-ministry-website-back-online/), [further source 2](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/250105/news/mandatory-slcert-recommendations-for-state-websites-583837.html)
- **Sources opened in full:** Primary source url opened 2026-11-11
- **Note:** The reported statements describe the website as it was before April 2024. They say nothing about the current website.
