# National government website research matrix

Generated by `scripts/build_reports.py` from `data/historical-incidents.csv` and `data/website-inventory.csv` and `evidence/evidence-register.csv`. Do not edit by hand.

This matrix shows, for each of the ten institutions, the public evidence found, what is unknown and where the research stands. It records past events and open questions. It identifies no current vulnerability in any website, and no request was made to any of them.

A historical incident does not show that a weakness exists today. Each entry states whether its sources were opened in full or read only as search-result excerpts; none has been saved yet, and each must be saved before it is cited.

Labels are defined in [evidence classification](../docs/evidence-classification.md). Research priorities are in the [confidence and risk matrix](../docs/confidence-risk-matrix.md). Incident details are in the [historical incident timeline](historical-incident-timeline.md).
