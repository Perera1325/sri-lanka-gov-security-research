### INC-20 — Ministry of Education

- **Label:** CONFIRMED HISTORICAL INCIDENT
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Incident date:** 2024-04-04
- **Publication date:** 2024-04-10
- **System concerned:** Website
- **What was reported:** The Ministry's official website was accessed without authorization and a message was posted on it.
- **Confirmed by an official body:** The Ministry acknowledged the incident in a statement reported on 5 April 2024 and apologized to users. Attributed to Sri Lanka CERT: Senior Information Security Engineer Charuka Damunupola, as reported by Daily Mirror on 10 April 2024, said CERT's initial investigation indicated a local individual was responsible and that a report would go to the Minister; Daily News (12 April 2024) reported him as saying the attack came from an internet connection within Sri Lanka. Daily Mirror (10 April 2024) also reports that SLCERT recommended launching a new website with updated security instead of reinstating the compromised one.
- **Reported allegations and unconfirmed details:** Unattributed: the 10 April 2024 Daily Mirror article states, in the passive and without naming a source, that security patches and updates had not been applied and that the Ministry had not carried out the annual vulnerability assessment SLCERT had advised. These are recorded as the newspaper's reporting, not as a CERT statement. The posted message claimed to come from a student and asserted security flaws; the author's identity was not established. A Ministry official told Ceylon Today that no Ministry information was compromised. Date discrepancy: the Ministry and Newswire give 4 April 2024; Daily News reports the CERT engineer as giving 5 April. Sri Lanka CERT's own statement or report was not found.
- **Official investigation announced:** The Ministry's statement, reported on 5 April 2024, said Sri Lanka CERT and the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission were among those conducting a probe. On 10 April 2024 CERT was reported as saying its report would go to the Minister; Daily News (12 April 2024) reported that steps were being taken to submit it to the CID.
- **Investigation outcome:** Unknown
- **Remediation confirmed:** A Ministry official said the site was restored on 5 April 2024. On 10 April 2024 the site was reported as due to return with basic features. Whether a new website was later launched, as CERT recommended, was not found.
- **Source type:** News report of official statement
- **Sources:** [source](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/SLCERT-engineers-reports-to-be-sent-to-Education-Minister-today/108-280550), [supporting source](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Probe-underway-into-breach-of-Education-Ministry-website/108-280302), [further source 1](https://www.newswire.lk/u3f4), [further source 2](https://www.newswire.lk/2xcd), [further source 3](https://www.dailynews.lk/2024/04/12/local/493369/slcert-confirms-cyber-attack-was-from-local-internet-connection/), [further source 4](https://ceylontoday.lk/2024/04/06/education-ministry-website-back-online/), [further source 5](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/250105/news/mandatory-slcert-recommendations-for-state-websites-583837.html)
