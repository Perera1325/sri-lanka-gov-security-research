# Template: notification to an institution

Use after Sri Lanka CERT has acknowledged the report, or as CERT advises. Send only to a contact published on the institution's own official website.

---

**Subject:** Security observation on your public website [domain]

Dear [title / team name],

I am an independent cybersecurity researcher based in Sri Lanka. While reviewing publicly accessible government websites using passive, non-intrusive methods, I noticed a configuration on your website that you may wish to review. I have also reported it to Sri Lanka CERT [reference number, if given].

**Public URL:** [exact URL]
**Dates observed:** [first date] and [second date]

**Observation**
[Neutral description.]

**Suggested fix**
[Specific remediation.]

I only viewed public pages in a standard web browser. I did not test, log in to, or submit data to your systems, and I did not access any personal or confidential information. I have not published these details.

I am not asking for anything in return. If it would help, I can send the supporting screenshots or explain further.

Yours faithfully,
[Full name]
[Contact email]

---

## Before sending

- [ ] CERT has acknowledged the report or advised direct contact
- [ ] The recipient address comes from the institution's own verified domain
- [ ] No personal data, credentials, exploit steps or payloads are included
- [ ] The message is being sent manually
